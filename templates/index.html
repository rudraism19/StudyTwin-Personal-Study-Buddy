Personalized Study Strategy Topic
👨‍🏫

The Feynman Technique

Explain what you just learned in plain words as if teaching your best friend. If you hit a wall or have to use textbook jargon, that is your exact blind spot. Re-read just that part!

Retention: 90%
⏱️

25/5 Pomodoro Cadence

Study for 25 distraction-free minutes (phone out of sight), followed by 5 minutes of stretching. Your brain consolidates short-term memories during rest intervals.

Focus Booster
📝

The Blurting Method

Read a topic for 15 minutes. Close the book, take a blank sheet of paper, and scribble down everything you can recall. Check against your notes in red pen.

Active Recall
📅

Spaced Repetition

Review key definitions at Day 1, Day 3, and Day 7. This flattens Ebbinghaus's Forgetting Curve and moves concepts into permanent memory before exams.

Long-Term Memory
